# Sales Review — v0.5

Track qualified leads, discovery calls, proposals, won/lost/deferred/pending, sales-cycle length, objection themes, no-fit reasons and price concerns. Separate counts and customer statements from interpretation. State period, cohort definition, source, counts, exclusions and missing data.

## Metric definitions

- **Qualification:** leads meeting the declared Sales fit rule / assessed leads in the same cohort; unknown assessments reported separately.
- **Proposal win rate:** won / (won + lost) among proposals from the declared cohort with a final decision as of the cutoff. Report pending/deferred proposals and counts; this rate excludes them and may have maturity bias.
- **Stage conversion:** leads reaching the next stage / eligible leads in the same entry cohort, with observation window and unfinished opportunities disclosed. Do not divide this month's wins by unrelated leads acquired this month.
- **Sales cycle:** time from the explicitly chosen start event to the chosen commitment event for closed opportunities. Give count and median if calculable; report won/lost separately when useful. Open ages are not completed cycle lengths.
- **Discounts/exceptions:** actual quoted/accepted departures from known policy, their stated reasons and effect on scope/economics; do not infer causality from correlation.

Zero denominators are unavailable, not zero percent. With small counts, show the counts and limits rather than implying stable conversion probabilities.

## Diagnosis and output

Low conversion may indicate poor qualification, offer mismatch, price/value communication, timing or sales execution. Compare client-stated reasons with process observations; a rejection alone does not establish its cause. Recommend an informative next test or local improvement before assuming a business-model failure. Report repeated offer/price/boundary mismatch to Strategy with evidence and alternatives.

Example, hypothetical: one declined proposal provides a specific case to examine, not a reliable estimate of market willingness to pay.
