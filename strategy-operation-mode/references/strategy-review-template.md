# Strategy Review and Experiment Records — v0.5

## Weekly, lightweight
What changed? Which leads were qualified? Which objections repeated? What customer outcomes and delivery costs were observed? What is the bottleneck, and where was time actually spent? Produce a few useful next actions, not a compulsory new experiment or pivot. Adapt duration to the decision.

## Monthly
Review offer boundaries, pricing evidence, capacity and actual mode time allocation. Keep, change, stop, defer or collect more evidence. Distinguish execution defects, product optimization and strategic assumption changes.

## Quarterly
Revisit ICP, positioning, alternatives, product portfolio and business economics when relevant. Avoid a pivot without supporting evidence; material new evidence can also justify revisiting decisions before the next quarterly review.

## Formal decision record

Question/time horizon | Context version | Evidence/date/source | Confidence and alternatives | Options/tradeoffs | AI recommendation | User decision or pending status | Decision owner/date/source | Effective constraints/affected modes | Next action/owner | Review date or event.

Do not fill a user decision by copying the AI recommendation. If policy is unchanged while testing, state that explicitly. For adopted changes, record the prior and new value, effective date, constraints and supporting decision; preserve history. Existing contracts do not change automatically.

## Experiment record
Hypothesis | Evidence today and baseline | Minimal test and audience/cohort | Signal and source | Proposed threshold and rationale | Timebox | Cost/time cap and authority | Stop conditions | Owner | Interpretation if success/failure/inconclusive | Actual result/evidence | Follow-up decision/status/date.

Before running a test, distinguish an approved experiment from a proposed one. Do not treat plan creation as authority to spend money, contact customers or publish. Existing explicit authorization remains valid; do not re-ask merely because a mode changed.

Success should justify the stated next step, not prove a whole business theory. Failure may challenge the hypothesis or execution. Inconclusive evidence can justify extending within a known cap, revising the test or retaining current policy. Missing results remain pending; never simulate outcomes and record them as observed.

## Cross-mode feedback requirements

Use comparable periods/cohorts, source and counts; distinguish measured facts, estimates, anecdotes and interpretations. Missing data is not zero. Check Marketing audience/qualified leads, Sales fit/closed and pending decisions, Delivery actual outcomes/time/direct costs, and founder capacity. Include contradictory signals.

When individual skills are unavailable, work from the supplied evidence and the field definitions above. Do not require installing another skill or assume it has stored data.

## Illustrative small-sample decision

One prospect declines on price and no other data exists. Possible explanations include fit, timing, value communication and price. Keep the adopted price unless the user chooses otherwise; propose a small, bounded investigation and state what evidence would justify reconsideration. No automatic repricing and no claim that one rejection validates a new segment.
