# Delivery Checklist — v0.5

## Before
Confirm scope, exclusions, expected outcomes, schedule/dependencies, communication, confidentiality, client responsibilities, acceptance and capacity. Distinguish agreed terms from proposals. Choose the smallest reliable plan and budget. Use [delivery-outcomes.md](delivery-outcomes.md) for acceptance and results.

## During
Track milestones, actual work, quality checks, deviations, client decisions and time/cost. Use the following record when economics or scope is material:

| Work item | Budget hours | Actual hours to date | Estimated hours remaining | Direct cost budget/actual | Scope change and impact | Decision/status/owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Record the actual item | Known budget or unknown | Observed only | Estimate and basis | Currency and inclusion basis | Scope, time, cost, outcome | Proposed or agreed, date/source |

Forecast hours = actual to date + estimated remaining. Show variance against the original or explicitly revised baseline. A forecast is not a completed cost.

For a change request: record request/source, current agreement, effect on scope/time/cost/outcome, options, proposed owner/decision, and new baseline only if agreed. Continue unaffected authorized work where feasible; do not silently add extras or remove commitments.

## After
Record acceptance/status, outcomes versus promise, actual effort/cost, useful improvements and evidence about the offer. Satisfaction and timely activity are not sufficient proof of the promised outcome.

## Economic definitions

- Record fee basis (contracted revenue, recognized revenue or cash collected), period and currency; do not interchange them.
- Contribution before founder labor = declared engagement revenue minus attributable direct costs. This is not net profit.
- Return per founder hour = contribution before founder labor / actual founder hours under the declared time scope. Include preparation, delivery, support and rework; disclose excluded acquisition/admin time. Zero or unknown hours produce no valid rate.
- If a founder labor cost is included, label the chosen hourly cost as an estimate/assumption and avoid subtracting it twice. Fixed overhead and taxes remain separate unless explicitly included.
- Compare forecast obligations with available capacity over the same time window; do not assume unallocated personal time is available for work.

## Escalation thresholds
Repeated scope creep, margin erosion, unmet outcomes or demand outside product scope go to Strategy with evidence. Individual agreement changes involve Sales and the relevant agreement. Use existing limits; otherwise propose a review trigger rather than inventing a universal overrun threshold. A single severe contractual/capacity risk can warrant immediate attention.

Illustrative calculation, not business evidence: fee 10,000, attributable direct costs 1,000, founder time 30 hours gives 9,000 before founder labor and 300 per founder hour under those assumptions. It does not establish net profit or future capacity.
