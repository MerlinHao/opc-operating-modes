# Marketing Operation Mode — v0.5

## Objective and scope

Make relevant customers recognize a problem and see a credible reason to engage with the business. Optimize for meaningful attention and qualified opportunities. Content is one mechanism; useful search answers, events, referrals and nurture sequences may serve the same objective.

Choose the primary mode by the task's intended outcome. A public lesson can be Delivery, and a livestream can combine Marketing, Sales and Delivery. Keep the primary success criterion clear while supporting secondary goals.

## Mode entry, context and exit

- **Entry:** infer the task, success criterion and habit to restrain from the request. Here, restrain premature frameworks, completeness inflation and neutral commentary without customer stakes. Ask only for missing information that materially affects the result.
- **Context:** use the current conversation or the user's designated business record for ICP, positioning, offer, price policy, promise boundaries, capacity and priorities. Mark facts, estimates, hypotheses, recommendations and user decisions separately. Do not invent missing evidence or silently change business policy.
- **Switching:** when objectives overlap, name the primary objective briefly if useful and continue all work already requested. Do not turn a mode change into a new permission gate. Park unrelated strategic issues with evidence; the entrepreneur owns business decisions.
- **Records:** use the designated location only when saving is requested or already authorized. Otherwise return a compact handoff in the answer; do not imply persistent or cross-session memory. A reusable record contains `date / mode / objective / context version / evidence and source / interpretation / result / decision status and owner / next action and owner / review trigger` as relevant. Unknowns stay unknown.
- **Exit:** deliver the requested artifact first, check it against the success criterion, and record material results, uncertainties and follow-ups. A small copy edit needs no full operating report. A handoff describes information and work; it does not itself send messages, publish or update another system.

## Choose the workflow that fits the request

### Insight discovery

For raw notes, observations or topic development, use **Capture → Cluster → Tension → Position → Gate → Package → Feedback**. Read [marketing-pipeline.md](references/marketing-pipeline.md) for the procedure and [marketing-gates.md](references/marketing-gates.md) when screening candidates.

Extract real signals: repeated pain, surprise, conflict and customer language. Separate observation, interpretation, proposed position and public expression. A single observation is not evidence of recurrence. No fabricated customer quotes, testimonials, results or conversion data.

For insight-led public content, **Relevance → Tension → Position → Credibility → Clarity → Depth** is a working heuristic, not a validated universal ranking. Credibility constrains every stage. Direct answers and practical instruction can be effective without controversy; use clarity and usefulness when the audience already knows its problem. Strengthen specificity and consequence rather than manufacturing conflict.

### Requested final copy

If the user requests a finished post, script, title or revision, produce it directly using the available brief. Apply insight checks internally; do not require a discovery session or a new framework first. Preserve the requested format and voice. Ask only if a material claim or requirement cannot safely be inferred; omit or qualify unsupported claims.

### Distribution and qualified opportunities

For campaigns, channel plans, calls to action, nurture or lead generation, read [marketing-lead-path.md](references/marketing-lead-path.md). Connect **audience → channel → promise → useful next action → qualification → Sales handoff → feedback**. Use the current offer; propose strategic changes separately.

For diagnosis of framework-first, teaching, coaching or research habits, read [merlin-anti-patterns.md](references/merlin-anti-patterns.md). These are optional personal working hypotheses, not fixed traits or universal rules.

## Outputs and checks

Choose the artifact the user needs:

- **Insight discovery:** signal and source, tension or practical need, provisional position, audience/stakes, possible packages, material evidence gaps.
- **Final copy:** the finished piece; add only material claim notes or assumptions.
- **Campaign/lead path:** audience, channel, promise, next action, qualification rule, measurement plan and experiment/review trigger.
- **Review:** observed response and qualified demand, interpretation with alternatives, next experiment. Likes alone are not qualified leads.

Before treating material as ready, check audience recognition, factual support, useful clarity, fit with business positioning, and an appropriate next action when conversion is part of the task. Keep a caveat wherever removing it would make the claim materially misleading. Anonymize client material; identifiable stories require permission for the intended use.

## Connections to other modes

Marketing is an Execution Mode in the 3+1 architecture. Send Sales only necessary context: source/content seen, expressed need, fit evidence, interest/contact permission, promise seen and open questions. Repeated wrong-audience attraction, positioning mismatch or product-level demand becomes an evidence-backed Strategy issue, not an automatic pivot. Strategy decisions update the operating context only when actually made or already delegated by the user.
