# Marketing Distribution and Lead Path — v0.5

Use for campaigns, channel selection, lead magnets, calls to action, nurture and marketing review. Scale to the task; one clear next action may be enough.

## Design the path

| Element | Decision to make |
|---|---|
| Audience | Which current ICP segment and live problem? What signals identify fit? |
| Channel | Where can these people be reached, and what evidence supports that choice? |
| Promise | What useful value does the communication offer, within current business boundaries? |
| Next action | Read, use, reply, register or request a conversation; choose an appropriate commitment level. |
| Qualification | Observable fit and intent conditions; distinguish unknown from failed conditions. |
| Nurture | What useful information answers the next question, with an appropriate contact basis? |
| Handoff | What makes this a concrete buying decision for Sales? |
| Experiment | Hypothesis, signal, source, time/cost limit and review/stop rule. |

Use current offer and positioning as the starting point. Proposed changes belong in a separate recommendation. A free download or like is not automatically an interested sales lead. Do not add an aggressive sales CTA when the user asked only for informational content.

## Qualification and handoff record

Record source/content seen, customer-stated need, ICP fit evidence, expressed interest, permission or basis for contact, promise seen, questions, and proposed next action/owner. Minimize identifiable client information. If permission or intent is unknown, label it unknown rather than claiming the lead is ready for outreach.

Marketing-qualified means the lead meets the explicitly chosen fit/interest rule; it does not prove Sales fit, budget or readiness to sign. Sales may refine that assessment.

## Metrics with usable definitions

For each metric record period/cohort, source, numerator, denominator, exclusions and unknowns. Preserve the platform's unit (people, sessions, accounts or impressions); do not mix it silently.

- **Response:** report reach/views and meaningful reactions separately. Engagement alone does not establish demand.
- **Action rate:** unique tracked actors completing the chosen next action / unique tracked visitors exposed to that action in the same window, if both are available. If only impressions are available, label that different rate explicitly.
- **Lead fit rate:** leads meeting the recorded qualification rule / leads assessed in that source cohort. Report unassessed leads separately.
- **Accepted handoffs:** leads accepted by Sales for a next step; include reasons for rejected/unknown handoffs.
- **Cost per qualified lead:** declared attributable campaign cost / qualified leads from that campaign/window. State whether founder time is included. Zero qualified leads gives no finite cost per lead, not zero cost.

Do not invent benchmarks. Compare against a known baseline or a proposed experiment threshold, with sample and attribution limits.

## Illustrative example, not customer evidence

A founder offers a fixed-scope onboarding workshop. A practical checklist helps the target customer assess their current process. The next action is an optional request for a fit conversation. Download count measures use of the resource; requests stating an onboarding need and matching the ICP become candidates for Sales assessment. No conversion or demand is asserted before observation.
