# Marketing Gates — v0.5

Use these gates to decide whether an insight-led communication deserves further development. For practical/search content, assess urgency, usefulness and clarity instead of requiring controversy. Credibility remains a constraint in either route.

## Audience

Ask:
- Is this experienced by the people the business wants to serve?
- Would attracting people interested in this topic improve the business's audience quality?
- Is this central, adjacent, or distracting to the intended positioning?

Failure pattern: intellectually interesting but attracts the wrong audience.

## Pain

Pain includes more than suffering. It may be:
- a costly problem;
- a recurring frustration;
- a difficult decision;
- a blocked aspiration;
- wasted time or effort;
- status/identity tension;
- fear of missing an opportunity;
- inability to convert capability into results.

Failure pattern: useful knowledge with no meaningful stakes.

## Tension

Look for:
- contradiction;
- counterintuitive outcome;
- trade-off;
- expectation gap;
- old belief meeting new reality;
- new capability creating a new constraint.

Failure pattern: a correct statement that creates no curiosity.

## Authority

Authority does not require being the world's leading expert. It requires legitimate grounds for the specific claim, such as:
- repeated first-hand observation;
- professional practice;
- personal experimentation;
- credible research;
- access to relevant cases;
- synthesis across domains with transparent reasoning.

Failure pattern: trend commentary far outside lived or researched territory.

## Decision Rule

Do not turn the gates into pseudo-scientific scoring unless a comparison requires it.

A strong insight-led candidate normally passes all four. A weak Tension gate alone does not disqualify useful educational marketing that serves the target audience. If a relevant gate is weak, diagnose whether to:
- collect more evidence;
- narrow the claim;
- reconnect it to a real audience problem;
- park the topic;
- choose a practical educational route if it serves a customer need, or classify it as personal learning if it has no current marketing purpose.
