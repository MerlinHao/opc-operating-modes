# Marketing Insight Pipeline — v0.5

Use this procedure for insight discovery. For requested final copy, use the brief and apply checks internally. For direct informational/search content, clarity and usefulness can lead without a contrarian position. Credibility constrains every stage; do not manufacture tension.

## Goal

Turn everyday business reality into a renewable source of marketing insights.

## Pipeline

### Capture
Record raw observations with minimal interpretation. Useful sources include customer conversations, coaching sessions, interviews, community discussions, comments, sales calls, delivery friction, product experiments, personal entrepreneurial experience, and industry events.

Record source/date where available; mark single observations, reported recurrence and verified recurrence separately. Capture especially:
1. Repeated Pain
2. Surprise
3. Conflict
4. Native Language

A capture item should usually be one to three sentences. Do not require it to be a topic.

### Cluster
Periodically group captures around the underlying problem. Look for recurrence across different people and contexts.

Good cluster names describe a problem or phenomenon, for example:
- capability surplus;
- productization difficulty;
- identity fragmentation;
- AI-enabled execution without commercial progress.

Avoid prematurely naming proprietary frameworks.

### Tension
Transform a cluster into a question with stakes. Useful patterns:
- X is getting easier, but Y is getting harder.
- People believe X causes Y, but repeated observations suggest Z.
- Two individually sensible behaviors create a bad combined outcome.
- A tool solves the old bottleneck and exposes a new bottleneck.
- What looks like a capability problem is actually a judgment/problem-selection/distribution problem.

### Position
State the current best judgment. It should be clear enough to disagree with and grounded enough to defend.

Position templates are aids, not mandatory formulas:
- “The real bottleneck is shifting from X to Y.”
- “Most people are optimizing X when the scarce resource is actually Y.”
- “X does not remove Y; it makes Y more important.”
- “The apparent problem is X, but the recurring underlying problem is Y.”

### Gate
Run Audience × Pain × Tension × Authority.

Reject or park ideas that are interesting but outside the business territory.

### Package
When discovering an insight, choose an appropriate format after clarifying the position. If the user has specified a format, keep it and adapt the insight to it.

Possible transformations:
- short video: one tension + one position + one concrete example;
- social post: observation → surprise → judgment;
- long article: phenomenon → evidence → mechanism → framework → implications;
- livestream: one central tension expanded into multiple scenarios/questions;
- talk: story → tension → thesis → framework → application.

### Feedback
Convert public response back into raw signals. Do not judge success only by reach. Track resonance, qualified conversations, objections, repeated questions, saves/shares, and whether the content attracts the intended audience. For conversion goals, use [marketing-lead-path.md](marketing-lead-path.md) to define the next action, qualification and measurement window. Public reactions are evidence of response, not proof of purchase intent or the truth of a claim.

## Suggested Cadence

Daily/lightweight: capture signals as they occur.

Weekly: cluster signals and develop a small number of tensions/positions.

Publishing sessions: package positions already judged worth developing.

Periodic review: identify which recurring positions are becoming durable content territories or signature ideas.
